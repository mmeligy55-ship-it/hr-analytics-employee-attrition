
# HR Analytics – Employee Attrition & Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
An HR Analytics project built using Microsoft Power BI to analyze employee attrition and workforce characteristics. The dashboard explores attrition patterns across job roles, job satisfaction, overtime, distance from home, years at company, and total working experience.

## Dashboard Preview

![HR Analytics Dashboard](HR%20Analytics.png)

## Key Performance Indicators
- **Total Employees:** 1,470
- **Total Attrition:** 237
- **Active Employees:** 1,233
- **Attrition Rate:** 16.12%
- **Average Age:** 37

## Analysis Areas
- Employee Attrition by Job Role
- Job Satisfaction vs Employee Attrition
- Attrition Rate by Overtime
- Distance from Home and Attrition
- Attrition by Years at Company
- Attrition by Total Working Years

## Key Insights
- Overall attrition rate is **16.12%**.
- Laboratory Technicians recorded the highest departure count (**62**), followed by Sales Executives (**57**).
- Low Job Satisfaction has the highest displayed attrition rate (**22.84%**).
- Employees working overtime show a higher attrition rate (**30.53%**) than those not working overtime (**10.44%**).
- The Far distance category has the highest displayed attrition rate (**21.40%**).
